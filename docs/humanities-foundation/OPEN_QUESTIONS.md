# Questions the cases leave open

**What does the risk colour ask someone to trust?** A reader can switch the same bell view between its photograph-like rendering, the released MS-L1 assessment and the synthetic source observations. The extra layer may invite closer inspection. Its definite colour categories may equally encourage deference to the model. The maps allow a comparison of these invitations. Distinguishing actual responses would require a separate reception study.

**Whose record counts as an account of the process?** The gallery manifest names the available source views. The recorded selection gives their query order. The Research Log explains why the team kept one target prediction fixed and varied the query. Why the designers never placed a camera somewhere else in the authored scene remains a separate question. [Research Log](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-aLBWAepKhZd6Wpv_CW1x8Hfds5-9DAePKEfhpftKak/edit)

**What would it mean to disagree with the image?** In the evaluation area one can dispute a colour label by recomputing a stated measurement at the same camera. A historical dispute might instead concern the date, use or status of the represented object. The gallery is authored, so changing its invented backstory could leave every RGB measurement intact. Resolving a historical claim would require different sources and expertise.
