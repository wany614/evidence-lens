# What produced the image and its risk score?

A reconstruction supplies a picture of a scene from a requested camera. A risk model examines that prediction and the available source material, then assigns scores to pixels. An evaluator can later compare the picture with a separate observation from the same camera. Only this last step measures the actual colour discrepancy. These three operations use different inputs and answer different questions. [Registered study](../research/many-source-v1/protocol.md) · [Worked examples and their limits](CONCEPTS_AND_CLAIMS.md)

## Which images enter each operation?

| Set | Role in the multi-source study | Example from the authored perforated gallery |
|---|---|---|
| **R** | The photographs used to match features, triangulate and colour the initial 3D points, and fit the Gaussian scene. | R16 and R128 produced two distinct reconstructions. |
| **A** | The distinct photographs the checker has permission to inspect. Every experimental library in this study used A = R. | The R128 reconstruction had 128 available photographs. |
| **Q** | The ordered subset of A that the checker actually consulted for one target. The record identifies the individual cameras. | Checking 4, 8, 16, 32 and then all 128 sources kept the R128 picture and camera fixed. |
| **H** | Separate target observations. The camera poses specify which views to predict. The independent target colours supply training labels, development comparisons or final evaluation according to the scene's assigned role. | Both reconstructions predicted the same six H cameras. Their confirmation images opened after the predictions and scores were sealed. |

In the controlled scenes a photograph is a Blender camera image of a designed world. In the ETH3D scenes it is a DSLR photograph. The bank supplies the permitted source photograph and a render made by the fitted model at that source camera. At the target it supplies the prediction. Each render contains display-sRGB colour (three channels scaled 0–1), expected depth along the camera's forward axis, and accumulated opacity (0–1). Depth and opacity let the checker project between views. They are estimates produced by the same model. The independent evaluator owns the target colours and, for the authored geometry tests, the original mesh. [Data roles, images and units](../research/many-source-v1/data/README.md) · [Feature definitions](../research/many-source-v1/feature-schema.json)

The multi-source study used a registered 378 × 252 centre crop and calibrated cameras with axes pointing right, down and forward. Authored depths and translations use metres. ETH3D retains its published frame in scene units with no verified metre conversion here. The project used the supplied ETH3D camera calibration, which may reflect the full capture. It matched and triangulated points afresh from each R set. Official scene points, tracks, scans and H photographs were excluded from the fit. The 15 fits used the same gsplat trainer revision. Its saved checkpoint follows 5,999 optimizer updates in 6,000 sampled training steps. ## What did the new head learn from?

Staggered arcade, terraced court and woven truss supplied all coefficient-fitting data. Their six reconstructions and 36 target-view instances produced 144,000 sampled pixel-and-query rows, including 6,833 positives. Different queries can revisit the same physical pixel. A positive means that the mean absolute difference of the three display-sRGB channels exceeded 0.05 on the registered grid. During development, layered louvers and ETH3D `delivery_area` selected the penalty and the simple residual comparison. The final fit reused only the training families. [Training and selection](../research/many-source-v1/model-card.md)

Each of the six authored families used 16- and 128-source fits and six target cameras. The DSLR scenes used 34 sources in `delivery_area`, 55 in `living_room` and 100 in `bridge`, each with ten target cameras. The last two external scenes and the two remaining authored families, perforated gallery and corner alcoves, supplied confirmation. Across these nine scenes the archive records 957 distinct source images and 1,053 memberships across reconstructions. It records 102 target predictions, counting a shared authored target once for each of its two reconstructions. The study's independent units are much fewer than its pixel or query counts.
