# Six physical DTU objects under the frozen reconstruction and risk models

Four source photographs reduced target RGB MAE on all six newly registered physical subjects. The object-equal mean fell from 0.20535 to 0.10053. The released MS-L1 threshold marked 41.70% of reference-valid pixels low in the four-source condition. Its mean within-object error rate among those pixels was 22.83%. A useful ranking did not provide the proposed 5% error operating point. [Per-object report and every denominator](confirmation/README.md)

On the current four-method intersection, four-source MS-ft had common-domain AP 0.6193 against 0.6052 for MS-L1. At the same 20% per-view coverage the mean accepted-group error was 16.94% for MS-ft and 21.05% for MS-L1. With two sources the ordering depended on the endpoint. MS-L1 had slightly higher AP (0.8185 vs 0.8172), while MS-ft had fewer errors at 20% coverage (40.94% vs 42.12%). The same six subjects, light, crop and camera layout produced all these comparisons. The default remains the previously selected lightweight MS-L1. The released fine-tuned weights remain available. No risk fitting, threshold selection or next-best-view experiment took place in this batch.
