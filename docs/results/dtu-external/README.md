# Frozen risk scoring on real DTU photographs

The four published methods ran on one external physical object from the official DTU SampleSet. The default MS-L1 showed a small amount of ranking transfer. Its released threshold marked few pixels low risk, and most of those pixels exceeded the registered RGB error tolerance. The risk parameters, feature schema and cutoffs are those of v0.3.0-rc.1 at `a65b1caea258d991573bddb7a65f2bc4f9945fd7`. The original synthetic confirmation and download remain available. ## Reconstruction before risk transfer

All four main reconstructions passed the published optimization checks and the new camera probes. M2 and M4 each completed 256 cyclic full-image updates over 4,096 fixed Gaussians. Each source therefore contributed 128 or 64 updates respectively. All saved source self-renders and target predictions matched their own final checkpoint. In scan6, 453 Gaussian rows in M2 and 497 in M4 changed at least one stored parameter. The two scan6 source self-render MAEs after M2 fitting were 0.09875 and 0.08466. The four M4 values were 0.12463, 0.12240, 0.12258 and 0.10268. Technically valid, poor target reconstructions created a high error base rate before the risk score was applied. The frozen rule for calling pixels low risk was unreliable on both conditions of this object.

A separate paired diagnostic on scan1 alone compared 256 and 1,024 fixed updates. M2 mean source MAE fell from 0.07457 to 0.06490 and six-target MAE fell from 0.26028 to 0.25535. M4 mean source MAE fell from 0.10202 to 0.08156 while target MAE rose from 0.20371 to 0.23422. Two additional scene fits and zero additional risk fits or scores produced this diagnostic.
