## The model’s training lineage

The formal research generator was Apple SHARP at commit `aed6527499ef91cba3b54c18d49a870f25947190`, checkpoint SHA-256 `94211a75198c47f61fca7d739ba08a215418d8d398d48fddf023baccc24f073d`. gsplat 1.5.3 rendered predicted RGB, optical-axis depth and opacity. The independent label compares the predicted target RGB against the matching Blender target and marks pixels whose mean absolute display-sRGB channel error exceeds 0.05. The target is independently measured rendering error. SHARP’s output supplies predictor inputs for that task. [Pinned SHARP software terms](https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-sharp/blob/aed6527499ef91cba3b54c18d49a870f25947190/LICENSE) · [Pinned model terms](https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-sharp/blob/aed6527499ef91cba3b54c18d49a870f25947190/LICENSE_MODEL)

The recorded fit `20261123T042239692597Z-0616eff18c3149a7` trained two L2-regularized logistic models from 393,216 sampled pixels in 16 original families. Of these, 120,458 were positive. Four separate development families set the operating thresholds. The optimizer fitted its own coefficients and intercepts. It inherited no SHARP weights, used no internal SHARP teacher targets and made no modification to SHARP’s architecture or training procedure. The frozen model ID is `20261123T042819219876Z-27eaa01eba29489d`. Its model index SHA-256 is `b3c65130bf803611e3904ac69a270f6944b48a8d78ce049bdc1424c15ba6cb54` and its training-manifest SHA-256 is `0e23b6dbc3c513b0a548c3ef164500d8bcfc1c8a65fe9bc9251c25b18026fbcb`. The ZIP includes the supporting records in `proofs/model-fit.json`, `proofs/development.json` and `training/training_dataset_manifest.json`. [Per-file model digests and versioned source evidence](../licenses/DEPENDENCY-SOURCES.json)

Apple’s pinned agreement defines model derivatives through modification or adaptation of its model, architecture, algorithm or training process, including retraining or fine-tuning. The project’s rights determination covers its separate error classifier and five exported JSON files. The reviewed agreement does not expressly extend its model-derivative definition to every independent model that uses outputs. Apple leaves users responsible for evaluating the appropriateness of using or redistributing those outputs. Its non-commercial scientific research restriction continues to govern running SHARP for the original research pipeline. The scope of redistribution rights for the raw scene predictions is the specific reason they are excluded. [Model derivative definition](https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-sharp/blob/aed6527499ef91cba3b54c18d49a870f25947190/LICENSE_MODEL#L17-L49) · [Output provision](https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-sharp/blob/aed6527499ef91cba3b54c18d49a870f25947190/LICENSE_MODEL#L64-L81)

## Authored examples and fixed third-party evidence

The tabletop demonstration uses original source image and camera calibration from Blender reference `20261123T023609071040Z-9fad7aa54fc84008`. Example derivation `20261123T034606860802Z-acb5e6955f424043` projected that image onto a two-metre plane. Scoring run `20261123T042828732686Z-a16179b8f5f343f4` evaluated those independent predictions with the frozen classifier. The project authored the scene geometry, materials, lights and camera arrangement. The reference images and plane example contain no SHARP-generated scene imagery. [Example notice](../licenses/EXAMPLE-LICENSE.txt)

The upstream evidence inventory records fixed SHARP software, model and acknowledgements files, gsplat’s license, dependency versions and actual notice-file hashes. The CPU wheel installs its two runtime dependencies separately. The model ZIP supplies that wheel and the separate model and example. Neither distributes the research renderer, CUDA libraries, Apple checkpoint, Blender, or numerical-library binaries. The full terms are accompanied by notices about separately licensed dependencies.
