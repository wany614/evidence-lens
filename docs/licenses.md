## Independent classifier

The recorded L1 fit trained two separate L2-regularized logistic regressions. Its features come from the source image, camera geometry and predicted RGB, depth and alpha. Its binary target marks a mean absolute RGB difference greater than 0.05 against the Blender target. The exported model contains its own coefficients, intercepts, preprocessing statistics and separately selected operating thresholds. SHARP supplies fixed prediction data. The downstream optimizer updates only the logistic coefficients and intercept. [Feature construction and deployment inputs](cpu-scoring.md) · [Evaluation implementation](../src/evidence_lens/labels.py)

Apple defines a derivative by changes to its model architecture, algorithm or training process, including retraining or fine-tuning. On the described facts, our working classification is an independently fitted downstream error predictor. Apple’s reviewed materials leave the status of this particular classifier and the redistribution license for its coefficients unspecified. Fit run `20261123T042239692597Z-0616eff18c3149a7` and training manifest SHA-256 `0e23b6dbc3c513b0a548c3ef164500d8bcfc1c8a65fe9bc9251c25b18026fbcb` record the 16-family fit and its input lineage. The fitted parameters and the upstream research model have distinct notices. A change that fine-tunes or adapts SHARP requires a fresh assessment against the definition. [Derivative definition](https://github.com/apple-aiml-research/ml-sharp/blob/aed6527499ef91cba3b54c18d49a870f25947190/LICENSE_MODEL#L30-L49)

## Original CPU example

Example derivation `20261123T034606860802Z-acb5e6955f424043` took the project's authored tabletop source image and camera calibration from Blender reference `20261123T023609071040Z-9fad7aa54fc84008`. The adapter projected that single image onto a plane at a fixed depth of two metres. It computed RGB, optical-axis depth and coverage alpha for the source and six nearby views on a 128×128 grid. All 16,384 source pixels had valid depth at 2 m. The first target had 15,714 supported pixels. Its predicted depth varied from 1.932 to 2.086 m. The adapter and example use original scene assets. Blender's artwork policy addresses creators' rights in rendered artwork. [Blender artwork policy](https://www.blender.org/about/license/#your-artwork)

Scoring run `20261123T042828732686Z-a16179b8f5f343f4` used the frozen model and this original prediction bundle. The example's separately recorded source, camera, prediction and method hashes are available in the release and source example. The formal quantitative evaluation reports SHARP-generated predictions on the registered family split. [Example provenance](../examples/tabletop-plane/preview.json)
