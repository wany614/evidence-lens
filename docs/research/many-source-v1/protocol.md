# A fixed-budget audit of multi-photo reconstructions

For a fixed reconstructed image, can four queried photographs miss comparable evidence elsewhere in the available source library? This study holds the Gaussian checkpoint, camera, target colour, expected camera depth and opacity fixed while varying the photographs available to the checker. Each target receives one ordered selection. RGB comparisons happen inside that selection. A separate full-library audit measures what the selection missed. [Registered study](../../../protocol/many-source-study-v1.json) · [Image-grid baseline](../../releases/v0.5.0/BASELINE_MANIFEST.json)

| Set | What the study records |
|---|---|
| R | The photographs actually used by source-side triangulation, colouring and Gaussian optimization |
| A | Unique photographs the study is permitted to query. The clean experimental banks use A = R |
| Q | The unique ordered sources actually consulted for the target at the requested budget |
| H | Independent target photographs that supply training labels, development selection or the final evaluation |

The six authored families use distinct geometries. Each has separate R16 and R128 reconstructions and the same six registered target poses. Staggered arcade, terraced court and woven truss supply risk-head training. Layered louvers supplies development data. Perforated gallery and corner alcoves supply confirmation data. The three ETH3D DSLR scenes are delivery_area for development, and living_room and bridge for confirmation. Ten targets per scene are selected by image identifier before reconstruction. Official calibration is an input condition and may reflect the complete acquisition. World translations and camera-Z use the stated scene frame. ETH3D records carry `scene_units` where the calibration has no verified metre scale. The official pixels use a corner origin; the converter subtracts one half from the principal point before applying A's centre-based crop transform. [Data and access registration](../../../protocol/many-source-data-v1.json) · [ETH3D datasets](https://www.eth3d.net/datasets) · [ETH3D camera conventions](https://www.eth3d.net/documentation)

Original and external inputs use the same pinned gsplat trainer, calibrated source-only matching and triangulation, and 378 × 252 analysis grid. The training images exclude every H photograph. The full official ETH3D sparse map, observation tracks, covisibility graph and scan are outside the reconstruction interface. The original geometry is available to the independent evaluator. The visual transfer model retains the original fitted DINOv2 parameters. The new ranking head is a NumPy logistic model with its own feature schema, preprocessing and training corpus. Its default output has no calibrated cutoff. [Official gsplat trainer revision](https://github.com/nerfstudio-project/gsplat/blob/937e29912570c372bed6747a5c9bf85fed877bae/examples/simple_trainer.py) · [Frozen prior models](https://github.com/wany614/evidence-lens/releases/tag/v0.3.0-rc.1)

**The P1 mechanism comparison** counts correctly predicted labelled pixels with no comparable selected source and at least one comparable source in the full audit. Divide by all correctly predicted labelled pixels in the target. The primary difference is coverage-first Q4 minus fixed Q4. A negative difference indicates fewer missed source opportunities. The audit is conditional on the reconstruction's predicted geometry.

**The P2 ranking comparison** takes the lowest-risk fifth of pixels scored by both the new head and a development-selected simple rule. The pair receives exactly the same Q16. Quotas round down per target and ties use row-major indices. Errors use mean absolute FP32 display-sRGB channel difference greater than 0.05. The report gives each numerator, the accepted count, the common domain and its share of the full reference image. Targets have equal weight within checkpoints, followed by equal checkpoint and scene weights. Limited positive support requires strictly lower accepted error on each of the two external confirmation scenes. [Complete endpoint and missing-value rules](../../../protocol/many-source-study-v1.json)

The remaining contrasts keep the changed factor explicit. Fixed, nearest and coverage-first choices use four sources. MS-L1, the genuine MS-ft and the new head share the same coverage-first Q4. The new head also uses nested budgets 4, 8, 16, 32 and all available sources. When budgets produce the same ordered prefix at the end of a smaller library, the reporting records the alias. Per-view source queries, data reads and the full audit have separate costs. Ranking quality, observed colour conflicts and evidence availability each have their own output.
