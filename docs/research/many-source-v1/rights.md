# Rights and provenance in the source-bank study

| Material | Use and distribution |
|---|---|
| Evidence Lens source, new CPU implementation and executable protocols | Original project software, Apache-2.0, attributed to wany614. |
| Authored scene observations, independent measurements, cameras and public demonstration | Original study material, CC BY 4.0, credited to wany614. The public demonstration uses an authored family. |
| ETH3D images, official calibration, and imagery derived from the benchmark | ETH3D licenses its data under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Original downloads, role-separated reference images, native renders and image-based external reports are held in the private study archive. Public research materials identify the originals and give numerical findings. |
| Third-party trainer and renderer | The pinned gsplat source retains its Apache-2.0 licence and upstream notices. The study records the exact revision, entry point and source-only adapter. |
| Legacy MS-L1, MS-frozen and MS-ft components | Existing model-release provenance and per-component notices apply. The MS-ft control uses the released fine-tuned parameters. |
| New logistic parameters and training-only preprocessing | wany614 grants Apache-2.0 for the project's original coefficients, imputation values and feature scaling. The three fitting families are original. One ETH3D development scene contributes to regularization and rule selection. The model card records both roles. |

ETH3D requested citation of its original benchmark publication. Thomas Schöps and colleagues describe the high-resolution multi-view collection in *A Multi-View Stereo Benchmark with High-Resolution Images and Multi-Camera Videos*, CVPR 2017. [ETH3D attribution and licence](https://www.eth3d.net/) · [Dataset download catalogue](https://www.eth3d.net/datasets) · [Research paper](https://www.eth3d.net/data/schoeps2017cvpr.pdf) · [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
