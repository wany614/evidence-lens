# Rights and data sources for the single-image vision study

The risk models learn to identify rendering errors. Blender reference images and externally rendered predictions supply measurements for supervision. The model input includes the predicted target RGB and 26 deployable numerical features. In training, Evidence Lens computes its binary labels by comparing predicted RGB to original Blender reference RGB at corresponding pixels. A public model can score a new scene without reference images.

Apple’s fixed model agreement restricts use of SHARP and its defined model derivatives to the stated research purposes. Its derivative definition covers changes to SHARP’s architecture, algorithm, training or model parameters. Its output provisions assign evaluation of intended use to the user. In this study, the SHARP generator runs inference under that agreement. Evidence Lens uses independently measured errors as targets and rendered pixels as inputs to a separate discriminative model. The only pretrained parameters in the distributed neural scorers come from Meta. This lineage is the basis for the project’s licensing decision for the risk parameters.
