Risk ranking on two held-out scenes

Both methods queried the same 16 sources for each target. Each accepted its lowest-risk 20% of their shared labelled, scoreable pixels, rounded down per view. A pixel counted as an error when mean absolute display-sRGB channel error exceeded 0.05.

Rates and percentage-point differences give each target equal weight within its checkpoint. The raw counts below pool pixels solely to show their denominators. The two scenes are separate confirmation cases.

What a missing rank means

A rank needs its method's required inputs. The earlier MS models can abstain when no queried source is comparable. B-CPU-LR can use missingness as a feature and still issue a rank. Source agreement, physical visibility and independent RGB error have separate measurements.

Physical visibility on authored geometry

The independent mesh test traces source lines of sight on a 54 × 36 grid. The table counts foreground probe samples where physical visibility and model-conditioned comparability agree or disagree, using coverage-first Q4. ETH3D supplied no independent mesh probe for the two external confirmation scenes.

“Only physical” means at least one selected source physically saw the true surface while the checker found no comparable source there. “Only model” means the checker found a comparable source and the probe found no clear line of sight. The dense nearest-depth approximation is a separate diagnostic.
