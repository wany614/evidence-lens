# Formal study figures

The four PNGs present numerical research results for four held-out families. The B0, R1 and Q1 charts show risk versus coverage for their respective conditions. The paired chart compares conditions on the same families. They contain plotted measurements and lettering. The public report gives the scope, methods and quantitative tables.
